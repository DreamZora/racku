main thread · wiring · swaps · supervisionruns no device
Replace any part of a running Bun program, while the rest keep serving.
Each part is a device in a Worker of its own, wired to the others through contracts.
⇄Hot swaps

New firmware starts beside the old, takes over calls in one step, and gets its state.

◎Faults stay put

A crash or a hang resets one device; watchdogs and bounded queues guard the rest.

↺Safe updates

An update that fails falls back to known-good firmware, and history() records it.
